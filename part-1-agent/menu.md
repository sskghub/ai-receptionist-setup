# Tierwell Cakes menu and shop details

Tierwell Cakes is a cake shop in Hyderabad.

## Cakes (price for 1 kg)

| Cake | Price per kg | Eggless available | Eggless extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chocolate Truffle | 700 rupees | Yes | 50 rupees |
| Black Forest | 650 rupees | Yes | 50 rupees |
| Butterscotch | 600 rupees | Yes | 50 rupees |
| Pineapple | 550 rupees | Yes | 50 rupees |
| Red Velvet | 850 rupees | No | - |
| Vanilla Raspberry (Tierwell signature) | 950 rupees | No | - |

## Sizes
- Smallest order is half kg. Half kg costs half the 1 kg price.
- Orders above 5 kg are bulk orders. The owner calls back about them.

## Writing on the cake
- A short message on the cake is free.

## Shop hours
- 9 AM to 10 PM, every day.

## Pickup and delivery
- Pickup from the shop is free.
- Delivery only within 5 km of the shop, 100 rupees extra.

## Notice
- Normal cakes: order at least 4 hours before.
- Custom designs: 24 hours notice.

## Payment
- The owner sends a WhatsApp message with UPI details after the call.
- The order is confirmed once half the amount is paid as advance.
- The shop never takes card or bank details on the phone.
